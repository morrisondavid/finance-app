Privacy notice

This site is operated for private use (owner and authorised operators only). By using bank account linking or automated feeds powered by Enable Banking, you should understand how data is handled.

What we process

Where you consent through your bank's authorised interface, we retrieve account information (including transaction histories) via Enable Banking acting as a technical aggregator under applicable open banking regulations. Processing is limited to what is needed to run this application (aggregation, bookkeeping views, exports you trigger).

Who receives data

  • This application — data is stored on infrastructure you control (e.g. your server or device) as configured by the deployment.
  • Enable Banking — facilitates the regulated connection between you and your bank.
  • Your ASPSP (bank) — holds your account data origin; access is gated by their consent flows.

There is no sale of personal data and no behavioural advertising powered by linked account data within this application. Any subprocessors beyond Enable and your bank should be documented by your deployment operator if they apply (e.g. cloud hosting regions).

Retention

Exported statements and ledger data retained on disk are governed by operator practice and backup policies. You may delete synced files and linked sessions on your deployment when you choose; consult your deployment guide.

Legal basis (UK/EU framing)

For regulated account information services, lawful grounds typically include consent given at your bank's PSU interface and legitimate interests where applicable to operate the application you run for yourself or your undertaking. This notice is illustrative; tailor it if you widen access beyond private use or change jurisdiction.

Contact

For queries about how your deployment handles data, contact the site operator hosting this instance. For Enable Banking-specific processing practices, consult Enable Banking's own documentation appropriate to your jurisdiction.
